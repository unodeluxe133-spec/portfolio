03 EXPERIMENTAL PROJECT

Studying the ageing of a lithium-ion battery.

As part of my TIPE research project, I studied how the state of health of a lithium-ion cell evolves, with the aim of statistically estimating the remaining cycles before it reaches end of first life. I combined cycling-data analysis with a Markov model, then built an experimental setup to monitor discharge and measure electrical quantities.
